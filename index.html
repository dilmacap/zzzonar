Frequently asked questions

How do I track a night?

Put your iPhone by the bed, ideally charging, open Zzzonar and tap Start tracking. In the morning, end the night and open your report.

Where are my recordings?

Recordings stay on the iPhone that made them; they are never uploaded, synced or backed up. Your nights' results sync through your private iCloud if iCloud sync is on.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone › your name › Subscriptions › Zzzonar. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I restore Premium on a new device?

Open the paywall or Settings › You and tap Restore, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I delete my data?

Settings › You › Data & privacy lets you delete recordings or all data, or export everything as CSV or JSON.

Is Zzzonar a medical device?

No. It is a wellness app and does not diagnose sleep apnea or any other condition. Talk to a doctor if you are worried.
